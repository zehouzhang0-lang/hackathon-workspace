Use case: ui-mockup
Create a realistic, production-quality Chinese desktop web app UI, full primary screen, high-fidelity design concept, not a marketing page. Direction name for the brief only: "纸面工作台". Do not print this direction name or an option number on the image.
Target dimensions: 1440 x 900. Current date: 2026-08-28, Asia/Shanghai. There is no need to display a date.
Product purpose: an easy business decision assistant for ordinary Chinese merchants asking "抖音做了不出单怎么办". This is the first of three independent pages: give a problem and optional existing material, compare choices, take away an action. Only the first page is shown. A sentence OR one material is enough. It does not diagnose sales causes on this screen.
Design a focused primary screen, not a feature inventory: one clear primary action and two input areas. Preserve all exact Chinese copy below. Body and form typography 16–18px, helper text >=12px, touch targets at least44px. Spacious, exceptionally readable, no giant hero. All controls will later be native HTML/CSS, not one screenshot.
Visual system: paper-white #FFFEFC canvas, deep ink #25241F, warm vermilion #D85E3A primary accent, hairline warm gray dividers, very faint cream input surface. Distinct editorial, calm, human, precise. Contemporary Chinese sans serif UI, one modest serif-flavored Chinese heading if legible, never calligraphy. Avoid dark mode, purple, gradients, shadows, card grids, pill badges.
Composition: a quiet top header with text product name left "抖音做了不出单怎么办", three-step horizontal navigation right "说清问题" (current, vermilion underline), "看清选择", "拿去执行" (muted). Thin bottom divider. Generous top and side margins. Main working area is open on the page, no encompassing app card. A moderately sized heading on the left reads "最近做抖音，哪件事最让你头疼？". Supporting text "一句话也可以，不用先懂数据".
Below: two deliberately unequal columns separated only by ample whitespace. Wide left writing area (about60%) labelled "用自己的话描述问题"; a generous 210px textarea with placeholder "比如：有人咨询，后来没下文". Under it, a quiet line "试试这样说" and two understated editable starter text buttons "有人看，但没人买" and "说不清卡在哪里". On the right (about40%), label "交一份手头材料", supporting "有相关截图或报表，也可以一起发来，不用提前整理". Elegant broad rectangular dashed upload dropzone with simple small outline upload icon, button "选择文件", and helper "也可以拖入文件，或粘贴截图". The upload region is the only dashed box. Underneath small format text "PNG / JPG / WebP / TXT / CSV / JSON", then "最多6份，每份5MiB，总计20MiB".
Bottom of main working area: thin divider; small explanation on the left "先说一句，或交一份材料，就能开始。" Primary vermilion action button on the right "整理一下" with restrained arrow icon, displayed in its disabled empty-input state without looking like a live success state. Very bottom left privacy/scope text "材料仅在本机处理；图片内容仍需核对。" Small bottom right "界面示意 · 尚未处理材料".
No uploaded sample, no made-up number or success status, no automatic diagnosis, no result cards, no chat bubble, no left sidebar, no login/avatar/search, no money figures, no reference-expression data injection, no business charts. Do not add additional navigation, marketing headlines, feature sections, eyebrows, badges, decorative illustrations, or device/browser chrome. Full page screenshot fills frame naturally without clipping. This concept only shows the first-step empty state, with all required input areas visible. Fonts, controls, spacing and palette must form a coherent reusable design language for later decision/action pages.
